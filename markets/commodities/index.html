Market considerations

What commodity traders watch.

Physical markets carry their own calendar: inventory reports, production meetings, harvest cycles and the contracts that expire beneath the instruments tracking them.

  • Supply shocks — outages, weather, policy decisions — can move prices faster than financial news.
  • The US dollar matters: most commodities are priced in dollars, so currency moves flow into commodity prices.
  • Instruments tracking futures markets can behave differently from spot prices around contract rollover.
  • Trading hours vary by market and often include daily breaks — check the schedule for each instrument.
