Companies you know, markets you can study.

Share instruments let you take positions on individual international companies — where earnings, products and guidance drive prices as much as the broad market does.

What share trading is

Single names, single stories.

Unlike an index, a share position lives and dies with one company. Earnings reports, product launches, management changes and sector rotation all land directly on the price — which makes shares the most story-driven instruments on the platform.

Share instruments follow exchange sessions, so prices gap between close and open, and quarterly earnings dates are scheduled volatility events worth marking on the calendar.

Company names appear for identification only. Dividend handling and corporate-action treatment are explained in the applicable instrument terms.
