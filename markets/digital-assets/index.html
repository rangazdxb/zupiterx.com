What digital assets are

Traded like markets, priced like sentiment.

Digital assets are blockchain-based instruments whose prices are set entirely by supply and demand on trading venues. There are no earnings, no coupons and no central bank behind them — valuation is a market opinion, and opinions change quickly.

Trading runs continuously, including weekends. That constant availability cuts both ways: markets can move significantly while you sleep, and there is no closing bell to pause a trend.

Digital-asset availability varies by jurisdiction and is confirmed during account opening.
