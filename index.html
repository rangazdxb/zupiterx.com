The essentials about ZupiterX, accounts, platform access and available markets.

ZupiterX brings multi-asset market access, platform tools and trading education together in one connected environment.

The platform is designed around five asset classes: forex, indices, commodities, shares and digital assets. Instrument availability varies by account and jurisdiction.

Yes. A demo account uses virtual funds in a simulated market environment, with the same platform tools as a live account. It is a practical way to learn order types, charting and platform behaviour without financial commitment.

Live accounts require identity verification before trading. This typically involves confirming your identity and address with official documents. The account portal shows the requirements for your jurisdiction.

Leveraged trading lets you open a position larger than the funds you commit to it. Profits and losses are calculated on the full position size, so leverage amplifies both. Our education section explains leverage and margin in detail before you consider using them.
