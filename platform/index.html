Platform tools

Built for the daily work of trading.

Chart tools

Candlestick and line rendering, multiple timeframes, and level-marking tools designed for study rather than spectacle. Settings persist per instrument.

Order tools

Market, limit and stop orders with stop-loss and take-profit levels attachable at entry — so risk is defined before the position exists.

Watchlists

Group instruments by asset class or strategy and scan sample prices at a glance. Your lists follow you across devices.

Alerts

Price-level notifications that watch the market so you don't have to. Alerts inform — they never trade on your behalf.

Mobile trading

The same market, in your pocket.

Watchlists, charts and sample position information from a responsive mobile experience — no installation required.

Security

Practices, not promises.

No platform can guarantee absolute security. Responsible account protection combines secure technology with careful credential handling by every user.

Encrypted sessions

Traffic between your browser and the platform is encrypted in transit as standard practice.

Authentication controls

Account access is managed through the secure portal, with clear sign-in and credential-protection controls.

Verification

Identity verification protects accounts and is required before live trading, subject to the applicable onboarding requirements.
